About this worksheet

Children write number names on cheques, forms and exam papers all their lives, and spelling them is a skill of its own. This sheet gives numbers to write in words, or words to write in figures, using Indian number names: thousand, lakh and crore.

Tips for parents and teachers

  • Spellings that need practice: forty (not fourty), ninety, eleven, twelve, fifteen, eighteen.
  • Numbers from 21 to 99 take a hyphen: twenty-one, sixty-five.
  • For big numbers, read period by period from the left: 4,52,318 is four lakh, fifty-two thousand, three hundred and eighteen.

Common questions

How do you write 1,00,000 in words?

One lakh. In the Indian system, 1,00,000 is one lakh and 1,00,00,000 is one crore.
