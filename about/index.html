About Worksheets

Worksheets is a free set of practice sheet makers for children from nursery to class 8: addition, subtraction, tables, fractions, clocks, tracing, writing paper, word search and more. You choose the settings, the page makes a fresh sheet with its answer page, and you print it or save it as a PDF.

Where the content comes from

  • The questions are made in your browser by our own program, from a sheet number. No question bank, book or other website is copied. The same sheet number always gives the same sheet.
  • All explanations and tips are our own original writing.
  • The maths follows the usual Indian school order and the Indian number system (thousand, lakh, crore). The class named on a page is a typical guide, not an official syllabus. Schools and boards differ, so follow your child's school.
  • Tracing sheets use the open-licence lettering Andika, Noto Sans Devanagari and Noto Sans Gurmukhi (SIL Open Font License), loaded from Google Fonts.

Answers

Answer pages are worked out by the same program that writes the questions. If you ever find a wrong answer, please tell us the sheet number printed at the bottom of the sheet.
