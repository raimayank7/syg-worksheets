How it works

  1. Open a worksheet, for example Addition.
  2. Choose the settings: size of numbers, number of questions.
  3. Press New sheet until you like it. Each sheet has its own number.
  4. Press Print, or save it as a PDF. The answers print on a second page.

Why make worksheets instead of downloading them?

A downloaded worksheet is the same every time. After one use, the child remembers the answers. Here every sheet is new, so the child practises the skill and not the page. You also decide the level: no carrying today, carrying tomorrow, twelve sums or thirty. A teacher can give every row of the class a different sheet of the same level.

The sheets are plain on purpose: black on white, large clear numbers, room to write, a line for name and date. They print cleanly on any printer and use little ink.

Common questions

Are the worksheets free?

Yes. You can make and print as many sheets as you like, at home or for a class. There is no login.

Do the worksheets have answers?

Yes. Every question sheet comes with an answer page that prints separately. You can untick it if you do not want it.

Can I use them in my school or tuition class?

Yes. Teachers may print them for their students. Use "Copy link" to share the exact sheet with parents.

Which paper size do they print on?

A4, the common size in India. They also fit Letter paper.
